🏛️ Academic Block

The main academic block houses all lecture rooms (GF-101 through GF-134), administrative offices, dean's office, seminar hall, and specialist facilities. The block is organized across two connected wings with central corridors and multiple washroom facilities for students and staff.

✅ Safe Routes

  • Main Corridor → Main Entrance
  • Left Wing Corridor → Hostel Exit
  • Right wing to main corridor → Main Exit
  • Ground floor direct paths to both exits

🚫 Blocked Paths

  • Electric Room areas — potential fire hazard
  • Chemistry Lab corridor (right side)
  • Rear staircase near service room

⚠️ Instructions

  • Leave all belongings
  • Do not use elevators
  • Help injured persons first
  • Assemble at Parking Lot
